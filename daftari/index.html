THE EDITORIAL SYSTEM

Stay consistent from the first announcement to the next edition.

Daftari is designed around the rhythm of event communication. Build a visual language once, then carry it across the campaign.

01

Before the event

Official announcements, save-the-date, event launch, artists and speakers, programmes, schedules, venue guides, ticket information, countdowns and registration notices.

02

During the event

Live updates, what's happening now, performer highlights, schedule changes, audience moments, behind-the-scenes coverage, quotes and official notices.

03

After the event

Highlights, photo collections, thank-you messages, attendance milestones, sponsor appreciation, event recaps, takeaways and next-edition announcements.
