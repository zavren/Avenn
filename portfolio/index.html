Terms & Conditions

1. Services

MtaaniWeb provides a done-for-you web presence service for small businesses in Kenya. This includes registration of a domain, design and build of a website, hosting, mobile optimisation, WhatsApp integration and basic search engine optimisation, delivered under the plan selected at the time of order.

2. Pricing and billing

Web presence service is billed on an annual subscription basis. Custom development projects are scoped and priced from KES 10,000 upward depending on the requirements agreed with you before work begins. Prices are subject to change for future renewal periods, and you will be informed ahead of any change affecting your renewal.

3. Domain registration and renewal

Your domain is registered using your business or personal details. MtaaniWeb manages the renewal of your domain on your behalf as part of the annual subscription. Domain registration remains subject to the rules of the relevant registrar. Failure to renew your subscription may result in the domain and associated website becoming inactive or unavailable.

Minor corrections are handled as reasonably requested. For larger changes, a Major Update window can be opened for a one-time fee of KES 800, which provides a 30-day period of unlimited changes to your website.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the business information, images and other content you provide for use on your website. MtaaniWeb is not responsible for losses arising from inaccurate or misleading content supplied by you.

6. Payment

Payment methods and instructions are shared with you directly via WhatsApp at the time of your order. Your website build begins once payment has been confirmed. Payments are non-refundable.

7. Delivery timelines

Typical delivery is approximately 24 hours from the point all required information and materials have been received. Complex development may take longer depending on scope.

8. Limitation of liability
