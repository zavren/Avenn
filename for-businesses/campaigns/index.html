CAMPAIGNS

How a Campaign Works

From the first brief to the final report, in seven clear steps.

Brief→Creative→Review→Approval→Publish→Distribute→Report

You tell us what you are promoting, a product, an event, an offer, or a launch. MtaaniWeb reviews it and proposes a service fee plus, where relevant, a separate advertising budget.

Once approved, the campaign runs across MtaaniWeb's own discovery surfaces and social channels, with optional paid advertising on top. You get results reporting at the end.

Editorial placement and paid placement are never confused: a campaign gets you distribution, it does not buy editorial credibility or verification.

What we need from you

A clear goal, the offer or event details, dates, your preferred channels, and any photos or videos you want used. Tell us your budget if you have one, and we will work within it.

What you get back

A written proposal, content for approval before anything goes live, clear labelling of sponsored placements, and a results report covering reach, views, saves and clicks where the platforms provide them.

Fees and ad budgets

The MtaaniWeb service fee and any advertising spend on Meta, TikTok or Google are separate line items. Where possible you keep ownership of your own ad accounts.

Standards we hold

Every campaign follows our Campaign Terms, Advertising Policy and Content Guidelines.
