Changelog

Every release of MtaaniWeb, newest first. Each version lists what changed and which parts of the site and admin it touched.
v1.1.0Design upgrade and events
November 2026
  • Business profile. Rebuilt in clear sections with budget, facilities and accepted payments, always-present action buttons and a visits tile.
  • Events. New event cards and page, separate venue and location, a Google Maps link, and an Events section on the homepage.
  • Cards. A divider row with people who visited, county and open status. Views and saves can be shown or hidden in Settings.
  • Footer links. Each footer link can be shown or hidden from Settings.
  • Verification. The audit trail on a request can be cleared or have single entries deleted.
v1.0.0MtaaniWeb Version 1.0
First official stable release · November 2026
  • Search. Right Now now lives on Search with the programme headline above it and two places per section. Trending searches (top 10, two columns) and the places people are checking out follow, then Explore. The search box types its own hints.
  • Homepage. A full-screen hero, Live on MtaaniWeb, the time block, This Weekend with pagination, Neighbourhoods, Explore and Essentials. Discover can be switched on from Page Content.
  • Events. Organiser, event type and event remarks (Cancelled, Sold out, Postponed, Rescheduled, Few tickets left, Free entry) on top of Published or Draft. Several images per event, a portrait poster on the event page, no repeated location, and collections for Today, Tonight, This week, This weekend, Free, Live music, Markets, Workshops, Festivals and Sports.
  • Pop-ups. Businesses, creators, posts and guides open in a pop-up first, with long descriptions, then lead to the full page.
  • Trust labels. Claimed and verified wording for businesses, creators and events.
  • Guides. Numbered sections, contents list, images in sections, tips, and a discussion block linked to Facebook, X and Instagram posts.
  • Business pages. Opening hours one group per line, Open 24/7 support, phone numbers shown with spaces, no repeated location.
  • Admin. A Right Now panel to edit sections and listed places, the changelog, weekend offers in Campaigns, page-section placement for media, pagination on long lists, and unsaved form entries kept on this device.
  • Reliability. The footer no longer flashes old content, pagination always shows at most three numbers, and filter chips are spaced consistently.
Work before this release was pre-release and is not versioned. Future updates continue from v1.0.1.
